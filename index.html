Project Abstract

Elagy is a smart e-prescription platform that provides a solution for the NFC emergency health tag system. It links doctors, patients, pharmacies, and emergency responders via mobile and IoT interface. In order to prevent prescription forgery, the doctor is obliged to confirm their Medical Syndicate ID and write their prescription by using templates or AI Speech-to-Text Engine in English language, standard Arabic and Egyptian Arabic dialects. Also, there is a clinical safety check in the system that checks for any drug interactions and allergies of the patient from the unified patient record system before writing a prescription. Patients have the ability to search for nearby pharmacies having available stock and get their prescription from any pharmacy chain within the participating pharmacies network after entering their ID number since they don't need a smartphone at all. Each patient will have a batteryless, waterproof Smart NFC tag together with hospital receptions and pharmacies which helps paramedics to get the necessary data of the patients instantly.
